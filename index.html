Step 1

Review email

The workflow pauses and emails the PM a preview with every automated check, plus an Approve / Reject form with comments.

Step 2

MCP: append_to_pulse_log

On approval only, the MCP server adds a formatted entry (newest first) to the Weekly Product Pulse Log Google Doc.

Step 3

MCP: create_email_draft

It then creates the Gmail draft "Weekly Product Pulse + Customer Clarification — [date]". It's never sent automatically.
